Hi, I'm Shreyans. I'm a quant trader at an options market maker. I'm from
Mumbai, moved to the Netherlands in November 2019, and live in Amsterdam.

## Why a blog?

Mostly to learn AI by building something with it. Making something real and
watching it work gives me the same eureka feeling as the first HTML file I
ever wrote.

I also wanted a home for my side quests. Writing things down gives what I
learn some structure, and it gives friends and family an easy way to keep up
with what I'm doing.

## What I write about

- [Running](/topics/running/): training logs, races and what I learn about
  building endurance.
- [Biking](/topics/biking/): rides, routes and gear.
- [Programming & AI](/topics/programming-ai/), [quant](/topics/quant/) and
  [finance](/topics/finance/): code and AI, quant trading, and personal
  finance.
- [Psychology](/topics/psychology/): notes on how minds work, mostly from
  books.

## Where to start

[I didn't write this website](/blog/building-this-site/) is about how this
site was made: an AI agent wrote the code while I steered. If you'd rather
